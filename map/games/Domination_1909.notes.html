== Domination ==
3 Player version All territories have production capacity. Each faction has a starting bid, with placement capped at the territories production value. Ships must be placed in sea zones that are adjecent to a controlled territory or island.

1909 - All territories have industrial complexes. Cheap units, lots of income, everyone gets a large bid to make each game unique. Marines and halftrack transportable. Destroyers can bombard. Extra capitals. Fighters are the only aircraft that kills other aircraft. The other aircraft are first-strike.
Strategy -
America should grow income.
Britain is the strongest country in the game and should pick a target and take a capital before America grows into a monster.
France should drop lots of units in the hexagon (mainland France) until it is safe from multi-sided attack. France has many pockets of territory across the globe that it will be unable to defend.
Spain should consider bidding a navy in the Philipines for a punishing first round strike on the weak Japanese navies.
